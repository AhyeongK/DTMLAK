Business Value

The proposed dashboard is intended to help teams move from scattered operational information to a clear and consistent support workflow.

01 Faster Identification

Highlight potential support needs before the same rework pattern continues across additional units.

02 Consistent Support

Apply the same recommendation logic across processes instead of relying only on individual judgment.

03 Faster Document Access

Connect an identified process need directly with the relevant TIS and OPL.

04 Focused Retraining

Recommend process-specific refreshers instead of repeating broad training for every situation.

05 Supervisor Visibility

Present key quality, training, and experience indicators in one decision-ready view.

06 Measurable Pilot

Support a future pilot that compares rework and training outcomes before and after implementation.

Proposed Implementation

A practical implementation would begin with a limited pilot and require validation by process, quality, training, and data owners.

01 Collect Approved Data

Use approved quality, rework, training, and experience records.

02 Review Indicators

Confirm meaningful thresholds and variables with process experts.

03 Recommend Support

Generate a process-specific refresher or supervisor review.

04 Connect TIS / OPL

Link the recommendation with approved work instructions.

05 Measure Outcomes

Compare rework, retraining, and process outcomes during the pilot.
